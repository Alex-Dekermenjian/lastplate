Sprint 1 demo guide

  1. LP-7 · Team 3

    Register and sign in as a shop operator

    Click Sign up and choose I'm a restaurant owner. Register a shop with a new email each run (for example live1@demo.lastplate.test). The workspace shows Pending review. Sign out, then log in again: restaurant owners land in their workspace.

    Sign up Log in

  2. LP-8 · Team 3

    Create and update the shop profile

    Fill in the pickup address, area and shop phone, then save. A shop needs a complete profile before it can be approved.

    Open shop profile

  3. LP-9 · Team 3

    Approve or reject shop accounts as an administrator

    Sign in as demo-admin. Open the new shop (or Zaatar Corner (demo)), choose Approved, tick the identity check and save. Optional: show that rejecting without a reason is refused. The admin belongs to the Marketplace service.

    Open shop review

  4. LP-10 · Team 2

    Create, save drafts and publish food offers

    Sign in as cedar@demo.lastplate.test. Publish the Cinnamon rolls draft, then create a new offer, save it as a draft and publish it.

    Open workspace Create food offer

  5. LP-11 · Team 2

    Listing photos and ingredient information

    While creating the offer, upload a JPEG or PNG photo and add ingredients and allergens. Customers see both on the offer page.

    Create offer with photo

  6. LP-12 · Team 2

    Manage shop offers and adjust available stock

    On Butter croissants, enter -2 in Adjust stock with the reason "Two boxes sold at the counter", then click Update stock. The stock goes from 6 to 4, and the change is recorded with its reason.

    Open workspace

  7. LP-13 · Team 1

    Browse available food and view offer details

    Sign out, click Sign up and choose I'm a customer (or log in as customer@demo.lastplate.test). Customers land straight on the food list. The newly published offers appear, the croissants show 4 left, and the pending shop's food is not listed. Open an offer to show the price, pickup time, address, ingredients and allergens.

    Sign up as a customer Browse available food

  8. LP-14 · Team 1

    Verify account permissions and input validation

    • Create an offer with a discounted price above the original price: it is refused with a clear message.
    • Sign in as the pending shop: it cannot publish.
    • Signed in as a customer, open the workspace: you are sent back to the food list.
    • Sign out and open the workspace: you are sent to log in.

    Try the workspace

  9. LP-15 · All teams

    Integrate, test and deploy the Sprint 1 demonstration

    • Four services (Accounts, Marketplace, Reservations, Web), each with its own database, all reporting ready on the home page.
    • Automated tests for every service run in GitHub Actions, plus a Docker Compose smoke check.
    • One command loads this demo: scripts/seed_demo.py.
